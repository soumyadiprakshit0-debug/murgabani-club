Our History

MURGABANI 1 TO 100 CLUB was established in 2013 with the vision of bringing people together through unity, culture, and social service. Since its beginning, the club has been organizing various religious, cultural, and community events, including Saraswati Puja and Laxmi Puja. Over the years, the club has grown into a respected organization where members work together to promote friendship, responsibility, and service to society. Every achievement reflects the dedication and teamwork of our members and supporters.

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯 Mission

To unite people through cultural, social and community activities while promoting teamwork, responsibility and service.

👁️ Vision

To become a leading community club that inspires unity, leadership and positive social change.

🤝 Core Values

Unity, Respect, Honesty, Service, Dedication and Teamwork are the values that guide every activity of our club.

Our Journey

2013

Club Established

Six young friends (aged around 10–15 years) founded MURGABANI 1 TO 100 CLUB. With the support of a few elder brothers, the club successfully organized its first Saraswati Puja despite financial challenges.

2014–2019

Years of Struggle & Growth

The club overcame financial difficulties through determination, community support, and teamwork. Every year strengthened the club's foundation and unity.

2020

Laxmi Puja Begins

The club proudly started organizing Laxmi Puja, expanding its cultural and religious activities.

2021–2022

Growing Together

Membership increased and more people from the community actively participated in club activities.

2023

10 Years Celebration

Celebrated a decade of togetherness with a special Saraswati Puja and thanked everyone who supported the club's journey.

2024–2025

Building Future Leaders

Encouraging the next generation to join the club and develop leadership, teamwork, public speaking, and organizational skills.

2026 & Beyond

Looking Ahead

Expanding social service through blood donation camps, tree plantation drives, community welfare programmes, and developing a modern Club Management & Information System.
